Questions

Is it the same Zelpophai AI as the website?

Yes. Same account, same chats, same files — log in with your username and password. (Google sign-in opens in your browser, because Google doesn't allow signing in inside apps.)

Why does Windows warn me?

Windows warns about any program that hasn't bought a code-signing certificate yet. Click "More info" then "Run anyway". We'll sign the app in the future so the warning disappears.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Not yet. On iPhone and Mac, open the website and add it to your home screen or dock.
