Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information Zelpophai AI ("Zelpophai", "we", "us") collects when you use our website, the Windows and Android apps and Zelpophai Mini, why we collect it, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language. If anything is unclear, contact us (details at the bottom).

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your username, email address and password. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google.
  • Your conversations: the messages you send, the AI's replies, chat titles, folders and settings such as your preferred model or personality.
  • Files and content you add: files you attach to chats, documents you upload to Knowledge, projects in Coding AGT., reminders, and anything you ask the AI to remember (Memory).
  • Feedback and requests: messages you send through Feedback, Personal Requests or support chat.
  • Voice: when you use the microphone, your recording is turned into text so the AI can answer. We don't keep the recording after that.

Zelpophai Mini (Windows app)

  • When you press the Mini shortcut, the app takes a screenshot of your screen (or only the area you select) so Mini can see what you're asking about. The screenshot is sent to the AI to answer, and is not stored on our servers.
  • If you turn on the clipboard option, the text you copied is sent with your question.
  • In Do mode, Mini sends a new screenshot at each step and, to help it open programs, the names of apps in your Start menu. It only moves the mouse or types during a task you start, and you can stop it at any time.
  • Your Mini questions and answers (text only, no screenshots) are saved to a "Zelpophai Mini" chat in your account so you can find them again. You can delete that chat like any other.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information: which features you use and how often (for example, how many images you made today), so we can apply daily limits and improve the service.
  • Device and connection information: your IP address, browser or app version, and a random device identifier stored in your browser. We use these to keep accounts secure (for example, to stop repeated failed logins) and to prevent abuse of invites and free plans.
  • Approximate time zone from your device, so reminders fire at the right local time.

Payments

If you buy a paid plan, payment is handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We receive confirmation of your plan and a customer reference, but we never see or store your full card number.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the service: answer your messages, create files, images, videos and reminders, save your chats and run your projects.
  • To personalise answers when you choose to — for example Memory, Knowledge and personality settings. You can turn Memory off in Settings.
  • To run your account: sign-in, plans, limits, invites and rewards.
  • To keep Zelpophai safe: prevent fraud, spam and abuse, and enforce our Terms.
  • To support you and reply when you contact us.
  • To fix problems and improve features, using usage information.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your conversations to show you ads.

3. AI providers

Zelpophai's answers, images, videos and transcriptions are produced by third-party AI models. To get an answer, the content needed for that request (your message, recent conversation, attachments, screenshots or audio) is sent to the provider that runs the model. We route most requests through OpenRouter, which passes them to model providers such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others. Images may also be created by Pollinations. Web search answers use search-enabled models.

These providers process the data to return a result under their own terms and privacy policies. We choose providers that do not use API data to train their models by default, but we can't guarantee every provider's practices, so please don't share information you wouldn't want processed by an AI service (such as passwords, card numbers or medical records).

4. Who we share information with

  • Service providers who help us run Zelpophai: hosting and storage (Railway), AI providers (above), sign-in (Google), payments (Stripe) and email or transcription services if used. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Staff: a small number of Zelpophai administrators can see account details and messages you send to support, to help you and to stop abuse. Admins do not browse your chats for any other reason.
  • When required by law, or to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public or Zelpophai.
  • If Zelpophai is sold or reorganised, your information may move to the new owner, who must keep protecting it under this policy.

5. Things you choose to make public

When you share a chat or a Coding AGT. project, anyone with the link can see it. Shared chats show your display name unless you choose to hide it, and files you attached only show their name, never their contents. A shared chat is a snapshot: new messages stay private until you update the link. You can stop sharing at any time. Shared pages are hidden from search engines.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use a small number of essential cookies and browser storage items. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

  • Login cookie — keeps you signed in. It expires after a while or when you log out.
  • Invite cookie — remembers which invite link you used, so your friend gets credit.
  • Browser storage — remembers preferences such as theme, which tips you've seen, a device identifier and offline copies of the app's files (so it loads faster).
  • Google sign-in — if you use the Google button, Google may set its own cookies under its policy.

You can clear cookies and storage in your browser settings, but you'll be signed out.

7. How long we keep information

  • Your account, chats, files and memories are kept while your account is active, until you delete them.
  • When you delete a chat or file, it's removed from your account straight away and from our systems and backups within a reasonable time.
  • Security and usage records (such as login attempts and daily usage counts) are kept only as long as needed for security, limits and abuse prevention.
  • Guest accounts that are no longer used may be removed.
  • We may keep limited information longer when the law requires it, or to resolve disputes and enforce our Terms.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and edit your account, chats, Memory and Knowledge in the app.
  • Delete individual chats, files and memories, or clear all your history, in the app.
  • Turn off Memory in Settings.
  • Delete your account: contact us (below) from the email on your account and we'll delete your account and its content.
  • Get a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local data-protection law, such as the right to object to or restrict certain processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. We'll respond to requests within a reasonable time and may need to confirm your identity first.

9. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, signed login tokens, login-attempt limits and restricted staff access. No online service can be 100% secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep your login details private. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we'll let you know as required by law.

10. Children

Zelpophai is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you're under the age needed to agree to these terms where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

11. International transfers

Our servers and providers may be in countries other than yours. When your information is transferred, we take reasonable steps so it stays protected as described in this policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Zelpophai changes. We'll change the date at the top, and for important changes we'll tell you in the app (for example in What's new) before they take effect.
